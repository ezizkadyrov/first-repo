Here you can see my text card game written on Java.
Contact me to any edits you want to add.

How to install the game:
Simply open java file "Main" in your JDK and play it in the terminal.

Rules of the game:
You have 2 teams with 5 champions. Each team consists of random champions.
There are 4 unique champions: Warrior, Archer, Bruiser, Mage.
They have hit poins and attack damage. 1 attack damage takes 1 hit point from the other champion.
Every round you should pick 2 champions: one from team 1 and one from team 2. They fight against each other.
Champion that survives wins the round. You have 5 rounds and 5 cards.
You have to choose wisely what champions should fight, so you can win.

Instructions.
After you have your 5 champions and you see other 5 champions.
Enter number of the champion you want to pick, then goes "Space" and then the number of the opposite champion.
After the battle you repeat the process 5 times until the winner of the game is announced.
